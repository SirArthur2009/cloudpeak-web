About Landry

Landry comes from a line of Master and Senior Hunters, and it shows — he is incredibly smart and always keeps us on our toes. He loves to work, learns at lightning speed, and has an eager-to-please personality that makes him a joy to train.

Off the field, he is as sweet as they come. He wants all the pets and snuggles he can get, and he absolutely loves his toys. We are so excited to have him as part of our family, and we know his puppies carry that same wonderful combination of drive and affection.

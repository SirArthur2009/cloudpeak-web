About Mia

Our beautiful Mia — Silver Belle — has been one of the most beloved members of our family. Well-traveled across the western United States, incredibly chill, and endlessly devoted to her people, she is truly everything you could ask for in a Lab.

Mia is smart enough to enjoy a good-natured tease, affectionate with children of all ages, and remarkably patient with those who have special needs. Even in play she is gentle. She loves to run, retrieve, and keeps a sharp eye on the property — Sage Grouse, antelope, coyotes, foxes, and even the occasional sparrow are no match for her nose and instincts.

And she absolutely loves water. Pool, beach, or mountain stream — if it's wet, Mia is in it.

Now a full-time therapy dog: After a wonderful career as one of our finest breeding dogs, Mia has retired and stepped into her most meaningful role yet — serving as a full-time therapy dog for Leah's oldest child, who has special needs.

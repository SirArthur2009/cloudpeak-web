About Stella

Stella is our amazing girl, carrying the genes of the legendary Prince Dustin — the granddaddy of all genetics in the Stonewick Labrador line. She has proven to be incredibly sweet, easily trained, and happiest when she is with her people.

One of her favorite activities is curling up by the fireplace for a reading session — or more accurately, soaking up all the pets she can get. She loves to play fetch, explore new places, and is an amazingly fast learner with her commands. Stella has traveled with us across the western states and takes every new adventure in stride.

A note on her lineage: Stella is a Dusty × Nola grandbaby from the well-known Stonewick Labrador line. If you have followed Stonewick Labradors, you'll recognize the significance of Prince Dustin's genetics — and we are thrilled to have that heritage represented in our program.

Our story

We are a family that loves adventure! Our dogs travel with us regularly and have proven to be wonderful companions on the road — adaptable, easygoing, and at home in a variety of settings across the western U.S. Our children have grown to love and trust them deeply, and they have been a true asset in countless situations.

Our families provide many opportunities for our dogs to be well-socialized, engaged, and playful. We are confident that our dogs can form a deep connection with the right family, and we believe they will be a genuine asset to your home. It is our desire to share with you the joy and excellence we experience with our dogs every day.

Our puppies grow up surrounded by kids, new experiences, and the rhythm of everyday family life. Leah's three children are part of the journey from day one through homecoming day, and her nieces and nephews help care for them along the way. This hands-on approach ensures that every puppy is well-socialized and comfortable with children before joining their new families.

We use Early Neurological Stimulation from day three through day sixteen as part of our program, giving each puppy a strong developmental foundation, resilience, and healthy coping skills — so they are ready to thrive in any loving home.

A note on scam awareness

Due to a recent increase in puppy scams across the internet, we want to make it easy for you to verify that we are a real, legitimate family-run breeding program.

We encourage you to do your research before sending any money. Here's how you can verify us:

Video call us — we'll happily FaceTime or video call so you can see the puppies and our home in real time before any deposit.
Check our Facebook — our page has real photos, history, and families who have tagged us with their puppies.
Verify our dogs on Embark — our dogs have public Embark DNA profiles you can look up independently.
Look up AKC registration — our dogs are AKC registered. Registration numbers can be verified directly on the AKC website.
We are in Buffalo, Wyoming — you are welcome to arrange a visit to meet us and the puppies in person before committing.
We only accept Venmo, check, or cash — we will never ask for wire transfers, gift cards, or cryptocurrency. If a breeder does, walk away.
Call or text us with questions — we welcome questions and are happy to chat. Ask us anything. Legitimate breeders have nothing to hide.

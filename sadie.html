About Sadie

Silver Sadie is one of our newest and most exciting additions to the family! She is the daughter of Landry and Mia, carrying on Mia's wonderful heritage now that Mia has been retired. If you have ever wanted one of Mia's pups, Sadie is your answer.

Sadie is an outgoing, sweet girl who settled happily into her home with one of our family members. She is a joy to be around, and her pups will be a wonderful fit for any loving home.

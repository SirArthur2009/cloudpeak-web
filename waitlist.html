Waitlist

Reservations are filled in the order they are made. Here's everything you need to know before applying.

How it works

All reservations are filled in the order the reservation was made. If we do not have a puppy to fill your reservation from your intended litter, you will have the option of having your deposit returned or being bumped to the next litter — again, in the order determined by your original reservation date.

How to reserve your spot

1

Check availability

Visit the Available Litters page to confirm there is still room on the waitlist for your intended litter before submitting an application.

2

Submit your application

Fill out the Puppy Application Form. Be sure to indicate which litter you are interested in.

3

We will contact you

Once your application is reviewed, we will contact you to further discuss your future puppy and to arrange payment.

4

You're on the list!

Your reservation is confirmed in the order it was received. We'll be in touch throughout this whole process.
